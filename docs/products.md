# Vericore — Products

## MRO & safety supplies
Millions of SKUs across PPE (including a dedicated women's PPE line), hand tools,
abrasives, cutting tools, welding, safety and janitorial categories.

## Own-manufactured safety equipment (Encon)
Protective clothing, emergency eyewash / drench-shower units, safety storage cabinets.

## Vending & dispensing hardware
Point-of-use machines (CabLock, AccuCab) controlling high-value consumables at the
point of work — each transaction tied to employee, job function, department, cost center.

## Data products (the layer this repo demos)
- **Golden vendor records** — deduplicated, enriched, hierarchy-aware vendor masters
- **Hierarchy roll-ups** — parent/vendor/site relationships for spend reporting
- **Data-quality scorecards** — completeness, uniqueness, consistency, validity,
  timeliness, accuracy
- **Merge & match feeds** — D&B candidate matching with rule-based auto-merge thresholds
