# Vericore — Services

Vericore embeds into a customer's operation and runs the vendor-data layer of their supply
network end to end.

## Integrated supply
Vericore staff on customer sites running the entire MRO supply chain, backed by six
centralized procurement centers (North America + Europe).

## Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI)
Vericore owns on-site ordering and replenishment for storerooms and cribs.

## Customer-Managed Inventory (CMI)
Customer keeps control; Vericore provides scan tools and governed item/vendor data.

## Storeroom management (SaaS portal)
One web interface managing storerooms, vending machines, VMI and CMI locations —
inventory, warranties, point-of-use programs, order-approval hierarchies.

## Point-of-use vending
CabLock / AccuCab machines dispensing high-value consumables, restricted to authorized
users, every dispense event captured in real time.

## Digital procurement
Tailored e-catalogs, purchase-authorization controls, automation that blocks rogue
ordering.

## Safety services
Equipment inspection, testing & maintenance, rentals, certification, training and
consulting.

## e-Crib platform
Proprietary web + mobile platform: one searchable database of all customer MRO
inventory, interfacing with customer ERP/maintenance systems.
